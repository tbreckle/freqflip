## [Unreleased]

### Added
- `freqflip HZ PROCESS` switches a display to the refresh rate closest to `HZ` (within `--tolerance`, same resolution), returns once it is switched and restores the original rate in the background when the process (name or PID) exits
- Named processes may start later (`--start-timeout`); a short grace period covers games that restart themselves
- Tray icon with tooltip and menu showing target, original and current rate and the watched process; "Restore and exit" ends early
- Restores on Ctrl+C, SIGTERM and SIGHUP as well
- `--list` shows displays and their available rates, `--display` picks one, `--foreground`, `--no-tray` and `--log` for scripting
- Backends: Windows (GDI), macOS (Quartz, for app only), Linux X11 (XRandR), GNOME (Mutter D-Bus), KDE (kscreen-doctor) and wlroots (wlr-randr)
- Pixel-art logo, app icon (Windows `.exe` icon) and tray icon, generated by `images/generate.py`
- CI/CD with GitFlow releases and SemVer versioning via `scripts/version.sh`, taken over from B.L.A.S.T.
